About Aivora Techverse

Aivora Techverse is a business management and ERP software platform company based in Lucknow, building real, working ERP systems for real businesses — not templated products dressed up as custom work.

Our flagship, Aivora Gym ERP, and its connected CRM were designed and built end-to-end in-house: the database, the security layer, the automated emails, the AI assistant. It runs live today, handling real members, real payments and real renewals. Build once: one secure core. Scale everywhere: every gym, plan and branch managed from one CRM.
